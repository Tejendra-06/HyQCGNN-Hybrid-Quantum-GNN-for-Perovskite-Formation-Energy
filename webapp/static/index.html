    Models & method

    All three models were trained on the same 80/10/10 split of every ABO3 oxide in the Castelli perovskite data set (MatBench). Scores are on the held-out test set.

    Pipeline

    1. Structure. Known (A, B) pairs use the DFT-relaxed cell. Others use an ideal cubic cell whose lattice constant is fitted from Shannon radii (mean error ≈ 0.13 Å).
    2. Graph. 5 nodes (A, B, O, O, O) with 7 electronic features each; 20 directed edges with distance, Coulomb, electron-affinity and electronegativity features.
    3. Models. XGBoost on the flattened graph; a GENConv GNN with a linear read-out; and HyQCGNN, where the GENConv output is amplitude-encoded into 5 qubits, passed through a trainable RX/RY/RZZ ansatz and read out as ⟨Z⟩ values. The GNNs are trained gradient-free with Nevergrad (2,000 NGOpt iterations).

    Reading the results

    • Energies are DFT heats of formation per 5-atom cell (eV). Divide by 5 for eV/atom.
    • XGBoost is the most accurate and is the headline value. The GNNs are shown for comparison: they are the research subject, not the production estimate.
    • A compound in the training split was seen during fitting, so its error is optimistic.
    • Elements outside the 52 in the data set, and cells built from radii, are extrapolations. Treat them as qualitative.
    • 0.825 ≤ t ≤ 1.059 and Bartel's τ < 4.18 indicate a stable perovskite structure; neither is a thermodynamic guarantee.
